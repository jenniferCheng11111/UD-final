        About the data

        Where it comes from

        Restrooms come from NYC Open Data’s Public Restrooms dataset. Our server copies it into its own database, works out each restroom’s borough from the city’s borough boundaries, and gives every restroom an ID made from its name and location, because the dataset has none.

        What the statuses mean

          Colour is never the only signal: every status also has its own door shape and a text label. Missing information is shown as “unknown”, never as “no”.

          How community reports work

          Anyone can report a problem without an account. Reports are public and unverified. A report that says a restroom is closed or out of order shows it as “Reported closed” for 48 hours, unless the official record already says it isn’t operating. Older reports stay visible on the restroom’s page for a week.

          To keep things fair, one visitor can send one report per restroom per hour, and answer “Still true” or “Not true” once per report.

          Limits

          Opening hours are free text written by the city. We read the common formats and say “Hours unknown” rather than guess at the rest. Distances are straight-line, so use Directions for the real walking route.
